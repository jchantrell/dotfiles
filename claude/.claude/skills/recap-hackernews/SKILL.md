# Recap Hackernews

Go to [hackernews](https://news.ycombinator.com/news) and read through the top 30 posts and the 10 top comments on each to work out sentiment. Categorise them and write a short summary of the post and sentiment in this format:

```md
## {category}
- first post - short_desc
- second post - short_desc
```
